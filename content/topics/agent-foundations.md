title: Agent foundations
---

How do agents represent uncertainty and choose what to do? These entries introduce a connected set of concepts from partially observable decision processes.

- [[action|Action]] — a choice available to a decision-maker.
- [[observation|Observation]] — evidence received about a state.
- [[belief-state|Belief state]] — a probability distribution over possible hidden states.
- [[policy|Policy]] — a rule for selecting actions from available information.

Read the scope of each entry: these pages use a specific POMDP formulation. For a different meaning of belief, see [[theory-of-mind|theory of mind]]. For how an LLM system supplies information to a decision step, continue to [[memory-and-information|Memory and information]].
