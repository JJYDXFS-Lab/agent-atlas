What information is retained, retrieved, and available to a model now? These entries separate storage mechanisms from the input of an individual model call.

- [[context-window|Context window]] — model capacity and the context supplied on a call.
- [[agent-memory|Agent memory]] — retention, updating, and retrieval across interactions.
- [[retrieval-augmented-generation|Retrieval-augmented generation]] — generation conditioned on retrieved passages.

Information availability is distinct from what a user believes is available. Follow [[common-ground|Common ground]] and [[theory-of-mind|Theory of mind]] into [[communication-and-interpretation|Communication and interpretation]].

## Access and the organization of evidence

[[horizon-of-observation|Horizon of observation]] distinguishes available task activity from the knowledge and attention needed to detect errors or learn. [[asymmetric-access-to-interactional-resources|Asymmetric access to interactional resources (analyst term)]] asks how participants differ in access to an interaction's circumstances and relevance. [[organizational-communication|Organizational communication]] concerns what is reported or filtered before it becomes a decision premise. These entries extend the question beyond storage capacity without treating visibility, understanding, and acceptance as interchangeable.
